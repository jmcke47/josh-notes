Privacy policy — Notes widget

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes the Google Play version of Notes widget (com.josh.notes).

Notes widget is a notes app for Android. It is built as a personal project. We do not run ads, and this app does not include an analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests: jmcke47@gmail.com

What the app stores on your device

  • Your notes, including titles, body text, checklists, colours, pins, reminders, tags, and attached photos or voice memos you add. These are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone.
  • App settings (theme, reminder preferences, and similar).
  • Secret notes are encrypted on the device with a key that stays in Android’s keystore. We cannot read them. A secret note can only be opened on that same install.
  • App lock uses Android biometrics or your screen lock. Biometric data is checked by the system and is not collected or stored by Notes widget.

Uninstalling the app removes the local copy of this data (unless you have a backup you made).

Backups you choose to make

Backups are optional and started by you (or by an automatic backup you turned on).

  • Folder / file backup: you pick a folder on the phone or in another app (for example a folder a cloud app syncs). The backup is a zip of your notes and attachments. We don’t operate that folder.
  • Google Drive backup (Premium): if you connect your Google account, backups go to a private app folder in *your* Google Drive (`drive.appdata`). That folder is not shown as a normal Drive folder; only this app can read it using your account. We don’t get a copy on our own servers. You can disconnect in Settings. Disconnecting stops new backups; existing files stay in your Drive until you delete them (or we can document how: Settings → Disconnect, and/or remove the app’s access in your Google account).
  • JSON export from the menu is a file you save or share yourself.

Secret-note *text* is omitted from folder/JSON backups. Drive backups may include secret notes only in encrypted form, openable on the same phone.

Microphone and voice

The microphone is used only when you start dictation or record a voice memo, and only after you grant the permission.

  • On-device dictation (the free default) uses Android’s on-device recogniser where available. Speech is processed on the phone.
  • Vosk (a Premium offline engine) downloads a speech model once from the engine’s publisher (alphacephei.com) and then recognises speech on the phone. That download is a model file, not your recordings.
  • Voice memos are stored with the note on the device, and may be included in a backup you have enabled.

Notes widget does not send your recordings or dictated speech to our servers. We don’t operate a speech service.

Reminders and notifications

If you set a reminder, the app schedules an alarm on the device and may show a reminder notification. Notification permission is requested only when you set a reminder. Reminder text comes from your note.

Purchases (Play Billing)

Notes widget Premium is a one-time unlock sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. We do not see or store your card number. The app stores a local flag (and a purchase token from Play) so Premium stays unlocked offline. Restoring purchases asks Google Play whether you already own the product.

See Google’s payments privacy policy for how Google handles payment data.

Data we do not collect

  • No advertising ID, no ads, no trackers, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in this app.
  • We do not create a Notes widget account. There is no Notes widget server that stores your notes.
  • We do not sell your data.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your notes with other companies for advertising or marketing.

Data may be *sent by the app, at your request*, to:

  • Google Drive, if you turn on Drive backup (your own account).
  • Google Play, if you buy or restore Premium (purchase status).

Android, Google Play and Google Drive are provided by Google LLC, under their own policies.

You can also share a note or a backup file yourself through Android’s share sheet; that goes to the app *you* pick.

Children

Notes widget is a general-purpose notes app. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

Security

  • Notes live in the app’s private storage.
  • Secret notes are encrypted with a non-exportable keystore key.
  • Drive and Play traffic use HTTPS.
  • You can add an optional app lock so opening the app requires your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we aim to keep local notes under Android’s normal app sandbox.

Your choices and requests

  • You can edit or delete notes, empty Recently deleted, export or delete backups, disconnect Drive, and uninstall the app.
  • To request access or deletion of anything we hold, or if you have a question: jmcke47@gmail.com. For a typical install we hold no copy of your notes on our own servers; Drive files in your account are yours to delete.

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and the hosted copy.
